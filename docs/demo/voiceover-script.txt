This is Caprigo — a local-first agent runtime from b_Radford and Vibes-Coded.

On the product site, Caprigo is built for operators who need agents in their real workspace: Overview, Session, and Board.

Here is the live runtime. For this cut, Caprigo is chatting through OpenRouter on an NVIDIA Nemotron model, while our scrap baseline remains a PowerColor Radeon RX 580 on Ollama Vulkan.

Overview shows runtime health, one hundred seventy tools, and a focused AMD demo agent — ready for Lemonade and DevMaster.

In Session, the agent answers cleanly: Caprigo is local-first, AMD GPUs keep inference cheap and private, and the same stack can hybridize with OpenRouter NVIDIA models.

Board is the operator HUD — arrange agents, switch LLM or local scripts, and launch workflow crews.

Our RX 580 lab measures decode throughput on Polaris. That lived proof is the scrap baseline before ROCm and Radeon acceleration.

Caprigo is open source on GitHub, MIT licensed. Built by b_Radford for the AMD AI Developer Program.
